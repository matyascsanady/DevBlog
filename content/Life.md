# University
All my life I knew I wanted to be engineer. When it came to choosing a university I wanted to attend BME (Budapest University of Technology and Economics) since it has the most prestigious engineering courses in Hungary. I ended up with Civil Engineering over Architecture and it was the best decision I ever made.
## Bachelors (BME - CIV)
During my bachelors I had mixed semesters. The first 2-3 were rough. I had a hard time getting up to speed and completing all the subjects. In the 4th semester something clicked and started acing my classes was putting in the work. After this I never failed a class (only on purpose).

This whole bachelors forces the students to choose a field and in that field a specialisation, that's why the bachelors is so long (8 semesters). I choose the field of geodesy and land surveying since i liked these early subjects and hated the focus of the other fields (structural and infrastructure). At this point I wanted to work in surveying, but during my two semesters focusing on this field I realised it is not for me.

Lucky for me from this filed I was able to specialise towards BIM. During my three semesters I found my place in the AEC industry and found myself a summer internship at a major local design firm. First applied for a BIM internship, but since the BIM department were not recruiting at that point, they offered me a structural position. I ended up taking it so I could try out this field, even tough I never wanted to work in it. For the remaining semesters I was studying BIM at the university working as a structural intern, documenting and designing structures. I had some fun in this work but quickly got bored of it.

Nearing the end of my bachelors, I choose a thesis subject that is more BIM related and ended up diving deep into software development.
